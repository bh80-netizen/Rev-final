Rice Electric Vehicle Solar Racing

The people
behind Carrera.

One solar car. A team built across disciplines.

REV brings Rice students together to design, build, and prepare for the Formula Sun Grand Prix 2027. Engineering, business, operations, and storytelling all have a part in getting to the track.

Design and build

Mechanical Teams

Suspension & Dynamics Subteam

Develop suspension, steering, and braking systems that shape how Carrera handles. Connect mechanical design with vehicle dynamics and testing.

Handling · Steering · Braking

Chassis & Structures Subteam

Develop the structures that support the driver and vehicle systems. Work on composite construction, component mounting, and the practical details of fabrication.

Composites · Packaging · Fabrication

Simulations & Analysis Subteam

Use models to understand loads, airflow, and vehicle performance. Help the team compare design choices and turn analysis and test data into better decisions.

Structural analysis · Aerodynamics · Modeling

Design and build

Electrical Teams

Energy Storage & Battery Subteam

Develop Carrera’s battery pack and its monitoring and protection systems. Bring together cell selection, pack integration, and the work needed for safe, reliable energy storage.

Battery pack · Monitoring · Protection

Power Distribution Subteam

Connect the solar array, battery, and drivetrain through the car’s electrical architecture. Develop the power electronics, wiring, and protection that route energy where it is needed.

Power electronics · Wiring · Integration

Software & Systems Subteam

Develop embedded software, vehicle controls, and telemetry. Build the tools that help the driver and team understand the car and make informed decisions.

Embedded software · Controls · Telemetry

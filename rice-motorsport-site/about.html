Who We Are

We Build Engineers.
The Car Keeps Score.

Rice Motorsports Team is a student group building a single-seat solar race car for the American Solar Challenge. Electrical, mechanical and business all work on the same deadline, and the car is the thing that tells us whether we got it right.

Our Mission Is the People

We exist to turn out electrical and mechanical engineers who have actually built something that had to work in front of judges. Not a class project marked on a rubric — a vehicle that gets inspected, scrutineered and then driven a thousand miles by people who are responsible for it.

Behind them sits a business team that is treated as equally technical. Sponsorship, budgeting, logistics and outreach are the reason there is carbon in the shop and a trailer at the start line, and we staff them accordingly.
